# Screen Bump icon concept prompts

Generated with the built-in image generation tool.

## Shared brief

- Use case: `logo-brand`
- Asset type: polished iOS app icon concept for “Screen Bump”
- Full-bleed square composition with a bold, centered mark that remains readable at Home Screen size
- Warm ivory, deep charcoal, amber, and restrained sage palette
- Calm, premium, and non-shaming
- No words, letters, numbers, coins, hourglasses, phone outlines, traffic signs, red, watermarks, or pre-rendered rounded app-icon corners

## Concepts

1. **Soft ridge:** A single soft amber ridge rising across a calm charcoal screen plane, like a gentle speed bump for scrolling; dimensional vector-like 3D with a tactile enamel finish.
2. **Scroll ribbon:** A continuous warm-ivory vertical scrolling ribbon that rises over a small amber bump and continues upward; crisp, sophisticated, and lightly dimensional.
3. **Interrupted track:** A smooth vertical charcoal track interrupted by a friendly raised amber semicircle; flat editorial vector geometry with strong negative space.
4. **Raised paper:** A tactile paper surface with one rounded bump pushing upward from beneath and a pause-like crease implied only through light and shadow.
5. **Pause ripple:** A circular ivory ripple that reaches a small raised amber node, compresses, and continues; bold embossed depth without wellness clichés.
6. **Lifted tiles:** Two offset warm-ivory screen tiles with a small amber cushion lifting the upper tile; clean geometric 3D in ceramic and paper materials.
7. **Redirected line:** One vertical charcoal line bending smoothly around an amber pebble rather than passing straight through; ultra-minimal flat vector mark.
8. **Raised threshold:** A symmetrical charcoal doorway whose threshold rises into a gentle amber bump, expressing “you can continue, but make it deliberate.”
9. **Double hump:** One continuous thick line with a small amber hump followed by a larger ivory hump; bold, flat, human, and not cartoonish.
10. **Flexing bar:** A small amber sphere resting on an ivory horizontal bar that subtly flexes at contact; premium tactile clay and enamel treatment.
